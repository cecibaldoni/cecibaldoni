## Hi, I'm Ceci 👋

My PhD said Behavioral Ecology on the cover, but it mostly consisted of an ungodly amount of R scripts and datasets that needed rechecking five out of four times.
 
Certified [Carpentries](https://carpentries.org/) instructor, currently training to teach the trainers, which is exactly as recursive as it sounds. I run workshops and mentor early-career researchers, mostly around open science, R, statistics, personal knowledge management. 
I spent Eight+ years in academia before that, half of it at the [Max Planck Institute of Animal Behavior](https://www.ab.mpg.de/), where I worked on shrews through the PhD and a postdoc. These days I work in scientific communication for medical technology.
 
I am also skeptical of productivity gurus, which is an uncomfortable position for [someone who writes publicly about her own note-taking system](https://cecibaldoni.github.io/blog/). I write about it anyway.
 
I draw. Sketchnotes, workshop slides with more doodles than a slide strictly needs, and a handwriting that has got measurably worse since I started typing faster than I write (alas, I am also prone to typs). Most of what I make lands somewhere between **teaching** and **illustration**.
 
**What's here**
 
- Teaching and workshop materials, openly licensed.
- The source for my site, a Quarto build with custom SCSS that I have rebuilt more than once. I write there about note-taking and research workflows, mostly for PhD students somewhere in year three.
- Some shrew data + code madness.
- Small tools I built to fix my own problems, then cleaned up enough for someone who is not me.
  
Most of these started as a personal fix, so expect documentation that is honest rather than complete (feel free to yell at me in an Issue!).
