## Hi, I'm Ceci 👋

I teach researchers how to work better with their tools,
their data, and their notes.

Certified [Carpentries](https://carpentries.org/) instructor, currently training to teach the trainers. I run workshops and mentor early-career researchers — mostly open science, R, and personal knowledge management. Before this: eight years in academia, a PhD in Behavioral Ecology and a postdoc at the Max Planck Institute of Animal Behavior. These days I work in scientific communication in medical technology.

I also draw, and most of what I make lands somewhere between **teaching** and **illustration**.

**What's here**
- Teaching and workshop materials, reusable and openly licensed
- The source for my site, where I write about note-taking and research workflows for PhD students
- Small tools I built for myself and cleaned up for other people
